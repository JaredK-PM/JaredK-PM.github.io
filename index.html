AboutThe stretch between demo and production

New technology is easy to demo and hard to put into production at a bank. Between the two sit legacy platforms, vendor systems, data mapping, testing, and the risk, compliance, legal, and audit sign-offs. I've worked in that gap at JPMorgan Chase, Kind Lending, loanDepot, Washington Mutual, and IndyMac, and as a consultant for Microsoft, Disney, Bank of America, and Wells Fargo. The domains have ranged from mortgage and wholesale treasury to Microsoft's delivery methodology and Disney's consumer ticketing portal, and the technology from rules engines to LLM workflows.

I set the roadmap and make the build-vs-buy, vendor, and risk calls with executives, then stay close enough to write the spec and test the release. I'm highly technical, not an engineer: engineering teams build the production systems, and I set direction and validate their output. At JPMorgan I owned the Collateral vertical and kept Appraisal as my own product. At Kind Lending I ran the Executive Steering Committee and prototyped our intake front end in Cursor.

Since Kind Lending's restructuring, I've founded ApexPrime Products and have been building BRĒTH, a physical product venture (market research and custom design through prototype iteration, manufacturing, unit economics, brand, and launch planning, targeted for Q4 2026).
